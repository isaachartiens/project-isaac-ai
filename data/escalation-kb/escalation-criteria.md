# Escalation Criteria — When to Escalate to Tier 2/3

**Document Type:** Internal SOP
**Audience:** Tier 1 Support Agents
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01
**Version:** 4.1

---

## Purpose

Define clear criteria for when Tier 1 agents must escalate to Tier 2 or Tier 3. Escalation is not a failure — it ensures specialized handling for complex situations.

---

## Tier Structure Overview

| Tier | Role | Scope |
|------|------|-------|
| Tier 1 | Frontline Support | Standard INR, INAD, returns, billing, account issues within policy limits |
| Tier 2 | Specialist Support | Complex disputes, fraud review, seller performance, counterfeit claims, high-value cases |
| Tier 3 | Senior Specialist / Policy Team | Legal threats, media cases, policy exceptions, cross-department coordination |

---

## Mandatory Escalations to Tier 2

### Financial Thresholds
1. **Dispute/claim value > $1,000**
2. **Refund amount > $500 outside policy**
3. **Multiple refunds on a single order** (potential double-dip fraud)
4. **Chargeback received** — regardless of amount

### Fraud Indicators
5. **Buyer fraud pattern** — Buyer has 3+ INR claims on "delivered" packages in 12 months OR 3+ INAD claims with seller evidence of buyer fault
6. **Seller fraud indicators** — 2+ counterfeit claims in 90 days, fake tracking numbers confirmed, or seller-shipper collusion suspected
7. **Account takeover suspected** — Unusual login locations, rapid changes to payment/shipping, mass purchasing
8. **Payment fraud flagged** — OMS fraud alert on transaction, mismatched billing/shipping with fraud score > 70

### Specialized Case Types
9. **ALL counterfeit claims** — No exceptions. Tier 2 Counterfeit team handles all authenticity disputes
10. **Conflicting evidence** — Buyer and seller both provide compelling contradicting evidence
11. **International INR > $500**
12. **Extended INAD claims** — Any INAD filed outside the standard 30-day window
13. **Vehicle or real estate transactions**

### Seller-Related Escalations
14. **Serial non-shipment** — Seller has 3+ non-shipment strikes in 30 days
15. **Pattern dispute behavior** — Seller disputes 3+ INAD claims in 90 days with evidence of seller fault
16. **Seller requests account review** — Seller formally disputes a performance strike
17. **Seller performance score below threshold**

### Account & Security
18. **Account compromise confirmed**
19. **Identity verification disputes**
20. **Restricted account appeals**

---

## Mandatory Escalations to Tier 3

Route through Tier 2 for triage, but mark as Tier 3 priority:

### Legal & Regulatory
1. **Threat of legal action** — Any mention of lawsuits, attorneys, court filings, or regulatory complaints
2. **Subpoena or law enforcement request**
3. **Regulatory complaint** — BBB, FTC, state attorney general, consumer protection agencies
4. **Government inquiry**

### Media & Public Relations
5. **Media inquiry** — Any contact from a journalist or reporter
6. **Social media virality** — Complaint gaining significant traction online
7. **Influencer/VIP public complaint** — Verified public figure or account with > 100K followers

### Executive & Policy
8. **Executive escalation** — Case forwarded by an executive's office
9. **Policy exception request** — Requires senior approval
10. **Cross-department coordination** — Legal, PR, Product, or Engineering involvement needed
11. **Systemic issue pattern** — 5+ similar complaints about the same feature/seller/issue in 48 hours

## How to Escalate Properly

### Step-by-Step Escalation Process

1. **Complete all Tier 1 steps first** — Tier 2 will return cases that haven't followed procedure.

2. **Document everything** — Case notes must include:
   - All steps taken (in order)
   - Evidence reviewed
   - Communications with buyer and seller (summarized)
   - Your assessment and reason for escalation

3. **Use the Escalation Request Form** — Found in the ticketing system under "Escalate."

4. **Select the correct escalation category** — This routes the case to the right specialist team.

5. **Set urgency level:**
   - **URGENT (SLA: 2 hours)** — Account compromise, active fraud, legal threats, media cases
   - **HIGH (SLA: 8 hours)** — High-value disputes, counterfeit claims, seller performance issues
   - **STANDARD (SLA: 24 hours)** — Extended INAD, international INR, conflicting evidence

6. **Notify the customer** — Use template ESC-01 (buyer) or ESC-02 (seller). Do NOT promise a resolution timeline.

7. **Set ticket to ESCALATED status.**

---

## Common Escalation Mistakes to Avoid

- Escalating without following SOP — Tier 2 will bounce it back.
- Escalating routine cases — If the answer is in the SOP, resolve it.
- Not documenting steps — Tier 2 needs to know what you've already done.
- Promising outcomes — Never tell a buyer or seller what Tier 2/3 will decide.
- Escalating to the wrong team — Check the category carefully.
- Failing to notify the customer before transferring.
- Escalating duplicate cases.

## Quick Reference: Non-Binding T2 Consults

Use **#t2-consult** Slack channel for uncertain situations:
- Provide case number and 2-3 sentence summary.
- T2 responds within 30 minutes during business hours.
- This is guidance only — not an official escalation.
- Do NOT use for urgent matters, fraud, or legal threats.
