# INR: Item Not Received — Tier 1 Resolution Guide

**Document Type:** Internal SOP
**Audience:** Tier 1 Support Agents
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01
**Version:** 3.2

---

## Purpose

Standardized procedure for handling Item Not Received (INR) contacts. Agents must follow this guide in sequence before escalating to Tier 2.

---

## Prerequisites

Before beginning the INR workflow, verify the following in the order management system (OMS):

1. Order status is "Shipped" (not "Processing" or "Awaiting Fulfillment").
2. Tracking number has been uploaded by the seller.
3. The latest estimated delivery date (EDD) has passed.
4. The buyer has not already opened a duplicate INR case on the same order.

**If the EDD has NOT passed →** Do not open an INR case. Inform the buyer to wait until the EDD. Set a follow-up reminder for EDD + 1 day.

**If a duplicate INR case already exists →** Merge the tickets and continue working the original case.

---

## Step 1: Verify Tracking Information

1. Pull the tracking number from the OMS order detail page.
2. Input the tracking number into the carrier lookup tool (TrackFinder).
3. Review the last 5 tracking events.

### Decision Tree: Tracking Status

**Tracking shows "Delivered"**
- Buyer confirms received → CLOSE CASE.
- Buyer says not received → Wait 24-48 hours for carrier scan errors.
  - Package arrives → CLOSE CASE.
  - Package still missing → Go to Step 2.
- Buyer reports stolen → Go to Step 4 (Stolen Package sub-procedure).

**Tracking shows "In Transit"**
- Last scan > 48 hours ago → Contact carrier for trace request. Wait 5 business days.
  - Carrier confirms shipment moving → Update buyer, wait for delivery.
  - Carrier confirms lost → Go to Step 3.
  - No carrier response in 5 days → Go to Step 3.

**Tracking shows "Label Created" only (no movement)**
- Label created > 3 business days ago → Go to Step 5 (Seller Non-Shipment).
- Label created < 3 business days ago → Wait, update buyer, set follow-up.

**No tracking number uploaded**
- Order marked shipped > 2 business days ago → Go to Step 5.
- Order marked shipped < 2 business days ago → Wait, set follow-up.

---

## Step 2: Delivered but Not Received — Investigation

1. Ask the buyer to confirm the shipping address on file matches their current address.
2. Ask the buyer to check with household members, neighbors, and building management.
3. Ask the buyer to physically check around their property (porch, side door, garage, mailbox area).
4. Check if the shipping address was a PO Box, locker, or pickup point.

**If buyer confirms address is correct and package is still missing after 24-48 hours:**
1. File a carrier trace request through TrackFinder.
2. Wait 5 business days for results.
3. If carrier confirms delivery to the correct address → **Escalation Trigger: Carrier Trace Dispute** — escalate to Tier 2 for potential fraud review.
4. If carrier cannot confirm delivery → Proceed to Step 3.

---

## Step 3: Carrier Confirmed Lost or No Resolution

1. Verify the carrier has officially marked the package as lost OR the 5-business-day trace window has expired.
2. Confirm the item was marketplace-fulfilled (MF) or seller-fulfilled (SF).

**Marketplace-Fulfilled (MF):**
- Issue full refund to buyer immediately (including shipping).
- File internal claim with carrier (auto-generated by refund tool).
- Close case with resolution code: INR-MF-CARRIER-LOSS.

**Seller-Fulfilled (SF):**
- Notify seller via OMS that INR case has been opened.
- Give seller 3 business days to respond with proof of shipment, replacement offer, or refund agreement.
- If seller provides proof → Re-engage carrier trace.
- If seller offers replacement → Confirm with buyer, monitor delivery.
- If seller agrees to refund → Process refund, close case.
- If seller does not respond in 3 days → Force refund under Money Back Guarantee.

**Escalation Trigger: Seller disputes INR but tracking clearly shows non-delivery** → Escalate to Tier 2.

---

## Step 4: Stolen Package Sub-Procedure

1. Require the buyer to file a police report.
2. Request the police report number and filing date.
3. Verify the shipping address is residential (not business/locker).
4. Check buyer's account for repeated INR claims on delivered packages.

**Escalation Trigger: Buyer has 3+ INR claims on "delivered" packages in the past 12 months** → Escalate to Tier 2 Fraud Review before issuing any refund.

**If no red flags:** Process full refund under Money Back Guarantee. Tag case: INR-STOLEN-MBG. Close case.

---

## Step 5: Seller Non-Shipment

1. Verify seller has not uploaded tracking AND has not responded to buyer within 48 hours.
2. Send formal shipment reminder to seller via OMS (template: SR-01).
3. Wait 48 hours for seller response.

**Seller responds and ships:** Verify tracking, update buyer, set follow-up for new EDD + 1 day.

**Seller responds but does not ship:** Ask for reason. If seller offers refund → process and close. If seller promises to ship with no timeline → force cancel and refund.

**Seller does not respond:** Force cancel order and issue full refund. Apply seller performance strike. Close case: INR-SELLER-NO-SHIP.

**Escalation Trigger: Seller has 3+ non-shipment strikes in 30 days** → Escalate to Tier 2 Seller Performance team.

---

## Step 6: International INR Cases

1. Extend EDD wait period to 30 days (not standard 7-14 days).
2. Carrier trace requests take up to 10 business days.
3. If package held by customs → Notify buyer, wait 15 business days for customs release. If still held → Process refund, file carrier claim.

**Escalation Trigger: International INR with claim value > $500** → Escalate to Tier 2 International team.

---

## Escalation Triggers Summary

- Carrier confirms delivery to correct address but buyer insists not received (potential fraud)
- Buyer has 3+ INR claims on "delivered" packages in 12 months
- Seller disputes INR but tracking shows non-delivery
- Seller has 3+ non-shipment strikes in 30 days
- International INR with claim value > $500
- Buyer requests escalation to management
- INR case involves a VIP/Power Buyer account
- Any indication of internal fraud (seller-shipper collusion, fake tracking numbers)

**All escalations:** Use the Escalation Request Form. Include case number, OMS order ID, tracking number, carrier trace reference, and a summary of steps taken.
