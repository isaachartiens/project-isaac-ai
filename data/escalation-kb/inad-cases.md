# INAD: Item Not As Described — Tier 1 Resolution Guide

**Document Type:** Internal SOP
**Audience:** Tier 1 Support Agents
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01
**Version:** 2.8

---

## Purpose

Standardized procedure for handling Item Not As Described (INAD) disputes. INAD cases require careful evidence review and structured negotiation between buyer and seller.

---

## Prerequisites

Before opening an INAD case, verify:
1. The buyer has received the item (tracking shows delivered).
2. The buyer has contacted the seller at least once through platform messaging.
3. The delivery date is within 30 days.
4. No duplicate INAD case exists for the same order.

**If delivery date is > 30 days ago →** Check extended claims section. If not eligible, inform buyer the INAD window has passed.

**If buyer has NOT contacted the seller first →** Send buyer to contact the seller first. Set 48-hour follow-up. Do not open a case yet.

---

## Step 1: Categorize the INAD Claim

| INAD Type | Description | Common Examples |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Wrong Item | Item received is different from what was ordered | Wrong size, color, model, product |
| Condition Mismatch | Item condition differs from listing | Listed "New" but used, listed "Good" but damaged |
| Missing Parts/Accessories | Components missing from listing | Missing charger, remote, original packaging |
| Defective/Damaged | Item non-functional or damaged on arrival | Electronics don't turn on, broken on arrival |
| Counterfeit Suspected | Buyer believes item is not authentic | Fake logos, poor quality vs. genuine |
| Listing Misrepresentation | Listing details don't match actual item | "Fits all models" but doesn't, "waterproof" but isn't |

---

## Step 2: Gather Evidence

1. Request buyer upload photos of the item showing the issue.
2. Request photos of the packaging and included accessories.
3. Request a photo of the shipping label.
4. Pull the original listing from OMS (use Listing Archive tool).
5. Compare listing photos/description to buyer's photos.

**Escalation Trigger: Listing edited since purchase and current version contradicts buyer's claim** → Escalate to Tier 2 Listing Integrity team.

**If buyer cannot provide photos after 3 business days:**
- Defective/Damaged claims → Close case (no evidence).
- Wrong Item claims → Proceed cautiously, rely on listing comparison.
- Counterfeit claims → **Escalation Trigger:** Escalate to Tier 2 Counterfeit team.

---

## Step 3: Determine Fault

**Wrong Item:**
- Clearly different from listing → Seller error → Full refund + return shipping covered by seller.
- Buyer ordered wrong variant → Check if listing had clear variant selection. If ambiguous → Seller fault, full refund.

**Condition Mismatch:**
- Listed "New" but clearly used (photos confirm) → Seller fault, full refund + return shipping.
- Damage undisclosed beyond stated condition → Seller fault.

**Defective/Damaged:**
- Damaged packaging → Carrier fault, file carrier claim.
- Item defective → Check manufacturer warranty first. If within 30 days and no warranty, seller's responsibility.

**Counterfeit Suspected:**
- **Escalation Trigger: ALL counterfeit claims escalate to Tier 2 Counterfeit team.** Do NOT make authenticity determinations at Tier 1.

**Listing Misrepresentation:**
- Verifiable claim contradicted (e.g., "waterproof" but isn't) → Seller fault, full refund + return shipping.
- Subjective claim (e.g., "excellent quality") → Buyer expectation issue.

---

## Step 4: Open INAD Case and Notify Seller

1. Open the INAD case in OMS using the INAD Case Tool.
2. Select the INAD sub-type from Step 1.
3. Attach all buyer-provided evidence and archived listing screenshots.
4. Record the fault determination.
5. OMS will automatically notify the seller with a 3-business-day response window.

---

## Step 5: Facilitate Resolution

**Seller accepts fault and offers refund:**
- Full refund → Process refund, arrange return label, close case.
- Partial refund → Confirm buyer accepts. If not, proceed to full refund.
- Replacement → Confirm buyer accepts. If not, proceed to full refund.

**Seller disputes the claim:**
- Counter-evidence clearly refutes buyer → **Escalation Trigger: Conflicting Evidence** → Escalate to Tier 2.
- Counter-evidence does not refute claim → Proceed with buyer refund.
- Seller claims buyer damaged item (3+ times in 90 days) → **Escalation Trigger: Pattern Dispute Behavior** → Escalate to Tier 2.

**Seller does not respond within 3 business days:**
- Force refund under Money Back Guarantee.
- Apply seller performance strike.
- Close case: INAD-SELLER-NO-RESPONSE.

---

## Step 6: Return Processing

1. Generate return shipping label through OMS.
2. Cost assignment: Seller fault → Seller billed. Carrier fault → Marketplace absorbs. Buyer error → Buyer pays.
3. Set return deadline: 14 days from label generation.
4. Process refund after seller inspection window (3 business days after return delivery).

**Escalation Trigger: Seller claims returned item is damaged/different** → Escalate to Tier 2 Return Dispute team. Hold refund until resolved.

---

## Extended Claims

INAD claims after 30 days may qualify:

| Exception | Time Limit | Requirements |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| Counterfeit discovery | 90 days | Evidence of inauthenticity |
| Hidden defect | 60 days | Defect not visible at delivery |
| Vehicle/Real Estate title | 180 days | Documentation of title problem |

**All extended claims → Escalation Trigger: Escalate to Tier 2.** Do not process at Tier 1.

---

## Escalation Triggers Summary

- ALL counterfeit suspected claims
- Conflicting evidence between buyer and seller
- Seller has pattern of disputing INAD claims (3+ in 90 days)
- Listing edited since purchase and contradicts buyer's claim
- Seller claims returned item is damaged/different
- Extended INAD filing (beyond 30-day window)
- Claim value > $1,000
- Buyer or seller is a VIP account
- Any threat of legal action
- Media or social media attention on the case
