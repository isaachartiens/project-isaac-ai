# Shipping & Tracking Guide

Getting your purchases delivered quickly and safely is our priority. This guide covers all our shipping options, delivery timeframes, and how to track your orders every step of the way.

## Shipping Options

We offer several shipping speeds to match your needs:

- **Standard Shipping**: Free for orders over $35. Delivery in 3-5 business days.

- **Expedited Shipping**: $9.99 flat rate. Delivery in 2-3 business days.

- **Express Shipping**: $19.99 flat rate. Delivery in 1-2 business days.

- **Same-Day Delivery**: Available in select metro areas for eligible items. $14.99 per order.

- **International Shipping**: Rates calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.

## Order Processing Times

- **Standard Orders**: Processed within 1-2 business days.

- **Personalized/Custom Items**: Processing times vary by seller (check the product listing).

- **Preorders**: Ship when the item becomes available — you'll get an email with tracking at that time.

- **Large/Bulky Items**: May require additional processing time (1-3 business days).

## How to Track Your Order

Tracking your order is simple and keeps you informed:

1. You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number once your order ships.

2. Log into your account and go to **My Orders**.

3. Click **"Track Package"** next to your order.

4. View real-time tracking updates from the carrier.

5. Sign up for text notifications by enabling them in **Account Settings** > **Notifications**.

## Multiple Shipments

Sometimes your order may arrive in multiple packages:

- Items from different sellers ship separately with their own tracking numbers.

- Items from different warehouses may ship in separate packages.

- Backordered items ship when they become available.

- Each package will have its own tracking number, all visible in **My Orders**.

## Shipping Restrictions

Some items have specific shipping restrictions:

- **Hazardous Materials**: Cannot ship via air (Express/Same-Day).

- **Oversized Items**: May not be available for all shipping speeds.

- **International Restrictions**: Some items cannot be shipped internationally due to customs regulations.

- **Age-Restricted Items**: Require adult signature upon delivery.

## Delivery Instructions

You can add delivery instructions for the carrier:

- Leave delivery instructions during checkout (e.g., "Leave at front door" or "Deliver to back porch").

- For signature-required deliveries, you can authorize package release through the carrier's app.

- Schedule a specific delivery window for an additional fee in select areas.

## Missed or Lost Packages

If your package seems lost or was missed:

- **Package Marked Delivered but Not Found**: Wait 24 hours, check with neighbors, and look around your property. Carriers sometimes mark items as delivered before they arrive.

- **Package Lost in Transit**: Contact us after 7 days (domestic) or 30 days (international) with no tracking updates.

- **Package Stolen**: File a police report and contact us — we'll work with the carrier to resolve the issue.

## International Shipping

- **Customs & Duties**: Import fees may apply based on your country's regulations. These are calculated at checkout for most destinations.

- **Delivery Times**: 7-21 business days depending on destination.

- **Tracking**: Available for most international shipments, but may be limited in some regions.

- **Restricted Items**: Check your country's import regulations before ordering.

## Shipping to PO Boxes & Military Addresses

- **PO Boxes**: Standard shipping only (no Express or Same-Day).

- **APO/FPO/DPO**: We proudly ship to military addresses. Delivery times may be longer (7-14 business days).
