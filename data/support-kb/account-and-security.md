# Account & Security Guide

Your account security is critically important to us. This guide covers everything you need to know about keeping your account safe, managing your settings, and protecting your personal information while using our marketplace.

## Creating & Managing Your Account

Your account is your gateway to our marketplace:

- **Sign Up**: Create an account using your email address, Google, Apple, or Facebook.

- **Profile Information**: Keep your name, email, and phone number up to date.

- **Multiple Addresses**: Save multiple shipping addresses for easy checkout.

- **Payment Methods**: Add, edit, or remove payment methods in **Account Settings**.

- **Communication Preferences**: Choose how and when you receive notifications, newsletters, and promotional emails.

## Password Security

A strong password is your first line of defense:

- **Create a Strong Password**: Use at least 12 characters with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

- **Avoid Common Passwords**: Don't use easily guessable information like birthdays, names, or sequential numbers.

- **Unique Password**: Use a different password for your marketplace account than you use for other sites.

- **Change Regularly**: Update your password every 6 months for optimal security.

- **Password Manager**: Consider using a password manager to generate and store strong passwords securely.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Enable 2FA for an extra layer of protection:

1. Go to **Account Settings** > **Security**.

2. Click **"Enable Two-Factor Authentication"**.

3. Choose your preferred method: authenticator app, SMS, or email.

4. Follow the setup instructions and save your backup codes in a safe place.

5. Use 2FA every time you log in from a new device.

## Recognizing & Avoiding Scams

Stay alert and protect yourself from common scams:

- **Phishing Emails**: We never ask for your password or full payment details via email. If an email seems suspicious, don't click any links — report it to us.

- **Fake Customer Service**: Scammers may pose as support agents. Always verify you're communicating through official channels.

- **Off-Platform Transactions**: Never complete transactions outside our marketplace. You lose all buyer protection.

- **Too Good to Be True**: If a deal seems unrealistically good, it probably is. Research the seller and item before purchasing.

- **Urgency Tactics**: Scammers create false urgency. Take your time and don't be pressured into quick decisions.

## Account Privacy

We respect your privacy and give you control over your data:

- **Data Access**: Download a copy of your account data at any time from **Account Settings** > **Privacy**.

- **Privacy Controls**: Choose what information is visible on your public profile.

- **Browsing History**: Control whether your browsing data is used for personalized recommendations.

- **Data Deletion**: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data through **Account Settings** > **Close Account**.

- **Cookie Preferences**: Manage your cookie settings for a more private browsing experience.

## Device Management

Keep track of where your account is being accessed:

- **Active Sessions**: View all devices currently logged into your account in **Account Settings** > **Security**.

- **Log Out Remotely**: Sign out of any device you don't recognize or no longer use.

- **Login Alerts**: Enable email or text alerts for new device logins.

- **Trusted Devices**: Mark frequently used devices as trusted to reduce login friction.

## What to Do If Your Account Is Compromised

Act immediately if you suspect unauthorized access:

1. **Change Your Password**: Do this right away from a device you trust.

2. **Enable 2FA**: If you haven't already, turn on two-factor authentication immediately.

3. **Check Recent Activity**: Review your order history, messages, and payment methods for anything suspicious.

4. **Remove Unknown Payment Methods**: Delete any payment methods you didn't add.

5. **Log Out of All Devices**: Use the "Log Out Everywhere" feature in security settings.

6. **Contact Support**: Reach out to us immediately so we can secure your account and investigate.

## Account Verification

Verified accounts enjoy additional security and trust:

- **Email Verification**: Required for all accounts — check your inbox after signup.

- **Phone Verification**: Optional but recommended for account recovery and security.

- **Identity Verification**: Required for sellers and high-value transactions to maintain marketplace integrity.

- **Address Verification**: Confirmed addresses speed up checkout and reduce fraud risk.

## Security Best Practices

- Never share your password with anyone, including people claiming to be from our support team.

- Use 2FA whenever possible.

- Regularly review your account activity and order history.

- Keep your contact information current so you receive security alerts.

- Log out from public or shared computers.

- Be cautious of links in emails and messages — always verify the URL before clicking.
