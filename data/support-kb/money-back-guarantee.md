# Money Back Guarantee

Your peace of mind is our priority. Our Money Back Guarantee ensures that every purchase you make on our marketplace is protected from start to finish. Here's everything you need to know about our guarantee and how it works.

## What Is the Money Back Guarantee?

Our Money Back Guarantee is our promise to you that shopping on our marketplace is risk-free. If something goes wrong with your order, we'll cover:

- **Item Not Received**: If your item doesn't arrive by the latest estimated delivery date, you're eligible for a full refund.

- **Item Not as Described**: If the item you receive doesn't match the listing description, photos, or condition stated by the seller, you're covered.

- **Damaged or Defective**: Items that arrive damaged or defective are fully covered under our guarantee.

- **Fake or Counterfeit**: If you receive an item you believe is counterfeit, we'll investigate and refund you if confirmed.

## How the Guarantee Works

Getting your money back is straightforward:

1. **Contact the Seller First**: Try resolving the issue directly with the seller through our messaging system. Most issues are resolved at this stage.

2. **Open a Dispute**: If the seller doesn't respond within 3 business days or you can't reach a resolution, open a dispute through **My Orders**.

3. **Provide Evidence**: Upload photos, messages, and any other relevant information to support your claim.

4. **Our Team Reviews**: Our resolution team reviews the case within 48 hours.

5. **Resolution**: If your claim is valid, we'll issue a full refund, including original shipping costs.

## Coverage Details

Our Money Back Guarantee covers:

- **Purchase Price**: The full amount you paid for the item.

- **Original Shipping**: What you paid for shipping is included in the refund.

- **Return Shipping**: If you need to return the item, return shipping is covered for "not as described" cases.

- **Taxes**: Any sales tax paid is fully refunded.

## What's Not Covered

While our guarantee is comprehensive, there are some limitations:

- **Buyer's Remorse**: Changing your mind is covered under standard return policies, not the Money Back Guarantee.

- **Items Damaged After Delivery**: Damage that occurs after you receive the item isn't covered.

- **Customs Seizures**: Items seized by customs in international shipping may not be covered.

- **Failure to Follow Return Instructions**: If you don't follow return shipping instructions, coverage may be voided.

## Timeframes for Filing a Claim

Act promptly to ensure your claim is covered:

- **Item Not Received**: File within 30 days of the latest estimated delivery date.

- **Item Not as Described**: File within 30 days of delivery.

- **Damaged on Arrival**: File within 14 days of delivery with photos of the damage and packaging.

- **Counterfeit Items**: File within 30 days of delivery.

## Tips for a Successful Claim

- **Document Everything**: Take photos of items, packaging, and any damage immediately upon receipt.

- **Keep Communication on Platform**: Always communicate with sellers through our messaging system.

- **Save Packaging**: Keep original packaging until you're sure the item is as described and working properly.

- **Act Quickly**: File claims as soon as you notice an issue.

- **Be Honest**: False claims can result in account restrictions and loss of guarantee coverage.

## Return Process for Guarantee Claims

If your claim requires returning the item:

1. Wait for claim approval before shipping the item back.

2. Use the return shipping label provided (if applicable).

3. Pack the item securely with all original accessories and packaging.

4. Drop off the package at the designated carrier location.

5. Your refund is processed within 3-5 business days of the item being received and inspected.

## Escalation Process

If you're not satisfied with the resolution:

- **Request a Review**: You can request a second review of your case within 7 days of the initial decision.

- **Senior Team Review**: A senior resolution specialist will review your case within 72 hours.

- **Final Decision**: The senior team's decision is final and binding.
