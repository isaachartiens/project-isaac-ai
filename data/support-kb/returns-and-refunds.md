# Returns & Refunds Guide

Welcome to our Returns & Refunds center! We understand that sometimes things don't work out, and we want to make the return process as smooth and stress-free as possible. This guide covers everything you need to know about returning items and getting your money back.

## Our Return Policy

We offer a generous return window because we want you to shop with confidence. Here's what you need to know:

- **Standard Return Window**: You have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return for most items.

- **Extended Holiday Returns**: Items purchased between November 1st and December 31st can be returned through January 31st of the following year.

- **Final Sale Items**: Some items marked as "Final Sale" are not eligible for returns unless they arrive damaged or defective.

- **Custom/Personalized Items**: These are generally non-returnable unless there's a manufacturing defect.

## How to Initiate a Return

Getting your return started is quick and easy:

1. Log into your account and go to **My Orders**.

2. Find the order containing the item(s) you want to return.

3. Click **"Request Return"** next to the item.

4. Select your reason for the return from the dropdown menu.

5. Upload photos if the item is damaged or defective (this speeds things up!).

6. Choose your preferred refund method.

7. Print your prepaid return shipping label (if eligible).

## Refund Processing Times

Once we receive your returned item, here's the timeline:

- **Inspection Period**: 1-3 business days after delivery to our warehouse.

- **Refund to Original Payment Method**: 3-5 business days after inspection.

- **Refund to Store Credit**: Instant after inspection is complete.

- **Refund to Bank Account**: 5-7 business days, depending on your bank.

## Return Shipping Costs

- **Free Returns**: Items fulfilled directly by our marketplace include free return shipping.

- **Seller-Fulfilled Items**: Return shipping costs may vary by seller. Some sellers offer free returns, while others may deduct a restocking fee.

- **Damaged/Defective Items**: We cover return shipping for all damaged or defective items, regardless of the seller.

## Common Return Scenarios

- **Wrong Item Received**: If you received the wrong item, contact us immediately. We'll send the correct item and cover all return shipping costs.

- **Item Not as Described**: If the item doesn't match the listing description, you're eligible for a full refund including original shipping costs.

- **Changed Your Mind**: Items returned in original condition with all tags and packaging are eligible for a full refund (minus original shipping for seller-fulfilled items).

- **Item Arrived Late**: If your item arrived after the estimated delivery date and you no longer need it, you may be eligible for a return at no cost.

## Tips for a Smooth Return

- Keep all original packaging and accessories until you're sure you're keeping the item.

- Take photos of items before returning them, especially if there's any damage.

- Use the provided tracking number to monitor your return shipment.

- Don't send items back without an approved return request — unapproved returns may be refused.
