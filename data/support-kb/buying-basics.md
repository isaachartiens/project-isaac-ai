# Buying Basics Guide

Welcome to our marketplace! Whether you're a first-time shopper or a seasoned bargain hunter, this guide will help you navigate the buying process with confidence and ease.

## Getting Started

Before you start shopping, here's what you need:

- **Create an Account**: Sign up with your email address for a personalized shopping experience.

- **Complete Your Profile**: Add your shipping address and payment method for faster checkout.

- **Set Your Preferences**: Choose your preferred categories, brands, and price ranges for tailored recommendations.

- **Enable Notifications**: Get alerts for price drops, deals, and back-in-stock items you're interested in.

## Searching & Browsing

Finding what you want is easy with our search tools:

- **Search Bar**: Use keywords, brand names, or product numbers to find items quickly.

- **Filters**: Narrow results by price, condition, location, rating, and more.

- **Categories**: Browse organized categories and subcategories for easy exploration.

- **Saved Searches**: Save your favorite searches and get notified when new items match.

- **Wish Lists**: Create wish lists to save items for later or share with friends and family.

## Understanding Listings

Each product listing contains important information:

- **Product Condition**: New, Refurbished, Used — clearly labeled with condition details.

- **Seller Rating**: Check the seller's feedback score and read recent reviews.

- **Price & Shipping**: Total cost including shipping is shown before you commit to buying.

- **Return Policy**: Each listing displays the seller's return policy.

- **Q&A Section**: Ask the seller questions before purchasing if anything is unclear.

## Making a Purchase

When you're ready to buy:

1. Click **"Add to Cart"** to continue shopping or **"Buy Now"** for immediate checkout.

2. Review your cart and verify item quantities and conditions.

3. Select your shipping address and preferred shipping method.

4. Choose your payment method and enter any promo codes.

5. Review your order summary and click **"Place Order"**.

6. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately.

## Best Practices for Smart Shopping

Follow these tips for the best shopping experience:

- **Read Reviews**: Look at both positive and negative reviews for a balanced perspective.

- **Check Photos**: Review all product photos carefully, especially for used items.

- **Compare Prices**: Check similar listings to ensure you're getting a good deal.

- **Ask Questions**: Don't hesitate to message sellers if you need more information.

- **Check Seller Ratings**: Aim for sellers with 95%+ positive feedback.

- **Watch for Deals**: Follow our Deals page and sign up for our newsletter for exclusive offers.

## Cart & Checkout Tips

- **Cart Timeout**: Items in your cart are reserved for 30 minutes during peak shopping times.

- **Guest Checkout**: You can check out as a guest, but creating an account gives you order tracking and easier returns.

- **Multiple Items**: Combine items from the same seller to save on shipping costs.

- **Gift Options**: Add gift wrapping and personalized messages during checkout for eligible items.

## Buyer Protection

Every purchase is covered by our buyer protection policies:

- **Full Purchase Protection**: If your item doesn't arrive or doesn't match the description, you're covered.

- **Secure Payment**: Your payment is held securely until you confirm receipt of your item.

- **Dispute Resolution**: Our team mediates disputes between buyers and sellers fairly.

- **Money Back Guarantee**: See our dedicated Money Back Guarantee guide for full details.

## Leaving Feedback

After receiving your item, share your experience:

- Rate the seller on a 5-star scale.

- Leave a detailed review about the product and seller service.

- Upload photos to help other buyers make informed decisions.

- Feedback helps maintain our marketplace quality and community trust.
