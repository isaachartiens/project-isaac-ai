# Selling Basics Guide

Ready to turn your items into income? This guide walks you through everything you need to know to start selling on our marketplace, from creating your first listing to managing your growing business.

## Getting Started as a Seller

Setting up your seller account is quick and straightforward:

- **Create a Seller Account**: Sign up or convert your existing buyer account to a seller account.

- **Verify Your Identity**: We require identity verification to keep our marketplace safe and trustworthy.

- **Link Your Bank Account**: This is where your earnings will be deposited.

- **Choose Your Seller Plan**: Select between Standard (free) or Premium (monthly subscription with advanced tools).

- **Set Up Your Storefront**: Customize your seller profile with a logo, banner, and store description.

## Creating Your First Listing

A great listing attracts more buyers. Here's how to create one:

1. **Take Quality Photos**: Use good lighting and multiple angles. Photos are the #1 factor in buyer decisions.

2. **Write a Clear Title**: Include brand, model, size, color, and key features.

3. **Describe Accurately**: Be honest about condition, flaws, and included accessories.

4. **Set a Competitive Price**: Research similar listings and price competitively.

5. **Choose Your Shipping Options**: Offer free shipping when possible — it increases sales by up to 30%.

6. **Select Return Options**: Offering returns builds buyer confidence and increases purchase likelihood.

7. **Publish**: Review your listing and click **"Publish"** to go live.

## Pricing Strategies

Pricing your items right helps them sell faster:

- **Research Comparable Listings**: Search for similar items and see what they're selling for.

- **Consider Condition**: Price used items 20-50% below new retail prices depending on condition.

- **Offer Discounts**: Create multi-buy discounts (e.g., "Buy 2, Get 10% Off") to increase order value.

- **Use Best Offer**: Allow buyers to make offers, giving you room to negotiate.

- **Time Your Listings**: List items on weekends or before holidays for maximum visibility.

## Managing Orders

When you make a sale, here's what to do:

- **You'll Receive a Notification**: Email and in-app alerts notify you of new orders.

- **Process Quickly**: Ship within your stated handling time (1-3 business days recommended).

- **Print Shipping Labels**: Use our integrated shipping tools for discounted rates.

- **Upload Tracking**: Always provide tracking numbers — this protects you in case of disputes.

- **Mark as Shipped**: Update the order status so the buyer knows it's on the way.

## Seller Fees

Understanding fees helps you price profitably:

- **Standard Plan**: 10% final value fee per item sold (capped at $750 per item).

- **Premium Plan**: $29.99/month with 7% final value fee and advanced analytics.

- **Payment Processing**: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.

- **Optional Features**: Promoted listings and listing upgrades available for additional fees.

- **No Listing Fees**: You can list as many items as you want for free.

## Building Your Seller Reputation

A strong reputation drives more sales:

- **Ship Fast**: Buyers love quick delivery — aim to ship within 24 hours when possible.

- **Communicate Promptly**: Respond to buyer questions within 24 hours.

- **Describe Accurately**: Honest descriptions lead to positive reviews and fewer returns.

- **Package Carefully**: Protect items during shipping with appropriate packing materials.

- **Resolve Issues**: Handle problems professionally — a satisfied customer often returns.

- **Analytics**: See which listings perform best and identify trends.

## Best Practices for Success

- Respond to all buyer messages within 24 hours.

- Ship items within your stated handling time.

- Use high-quality photos (at least 1000x1000 pixels).

- Keep your inventory updated to avoid canceled orders.

- Offer returns to build buyer trust.

- Price items competitively and adjust based on performance.
