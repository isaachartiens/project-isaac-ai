# Payments & Billing Guide

We want your shopping experience to be seamless from browse to checkout. This guide explains everything about payments, billing, and keeping your financial information secure on our marketplace.

## Accepted Payment Methods

We support a wide range of payment options so you can shop your way:

- **Credit & Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and Diners Club.

- **Digital Wallets**: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay.

- **Buy Now, Pay Later**: Klarna, Afterpay, and Affirm for eligible purchases.

- **Gift Cards**: Marketplace gift cards and select retailer gift cards.

- **Store Credit**: Available from returns and promotional offers.

- **Bank Transfers**: Direct bank transfer for orders over $500.

## How Billing Works

Understanding your bill helps you stay on top of your purchases:

- **Authorization**: When you place an order, we authorize the charge but don't capture it until the item ships.

- **Partial Shipments**: If your order ships in multiple packages, you'll see separate charges for each shipment.

- **Preorders**: Payment is captured when the item becomes available and ships, not at the time of order.

- **Currency**: All prices are shown in your local currency. International orders may incur conversion fees from your bank.

## Managing Payment Methods

You can easily manage your saved payment methods:

1. Go to **Account Settings** > **Payment Methods**.

2. Add a new card or digital wallet by clicking **"Add Payment Method"**.

3. Edit or remove existing payment methods at any time.

4. Set a default payment method for faster checkout.

5. Enable or disable specific methods for recurring purchases.

## Understanding Your Invoices

Every order comes with a detailed invoice:

- **Order Number**: A unique identifier for your purchase (format: MP-XXXXXXXXXX).

- **Itemized List**: Each item, its price, and any applicable taxes.

- **Shipping Costs**: Clearly broken down by shipping method and destination.

- **Discounts & Promotions**: Applied coupons and promotional savings.

- **Total Charged**: The final amount charged to your payment method.

## Sales Tax

- Sales tax is calculated based on your shipping address and applicable state/local tax rates.

- Some digital products may not be subject to sales tax depending on your location.

- Tax-exempt organizations can upload their exemption certificate in **Account Settings**.

## Payment Security

Your financial security is our top priority:

- All payment information is encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology.

- We are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant — the highest level of payment security.

- We never store your full credit card number on our servers.

- Fraud detection systems monitor every transaction in real time.

- Two-factor authentication is available for all account changes.

## Buy Now, Pay Later Options

We partner with leading BNPL providers to give you flexible payment options:

- **Klarna**: Split purchases into 4 interest-free payments.

- **Afterpay**: Pay in 4 installments over 6 weeks.

- **Affirm**: Choose from 3, 6, or 12-month payment plans (interest may apply).

- Eligibility is determined at checkout based on your order total and account history.

## Common Billing Issues & Solutions

- **Payment Declined**: Check with your bank or try an alternative payment method. Your bank may need to approve the transaction.

- **Duplicate Charge**: Pending authorizations may appear as duplicate charges. These typically resolve within 3-5 business days.

- **Refund Not Received**: Refunds take 3-7 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

- **Promo Code Not Working**: Check expiration dates, minimum purchase requirements, and item eligibility.
